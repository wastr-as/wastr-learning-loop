# Lens: funder (grant or investor)

> Type-level frame. Hypotheses; the Innovasjon Norge reporting boundary comes from the README.
> Refine from real notes; a stakeholder's own folder always outranks this file.

## Who this is

An organisation that funds WASTR (a public grant body such as Innovasjon Norge, an accelerator or an
investor) and judges progress against what it funded.

## What they decide

Whether to keep funding, release the next tranche, accept a report, or invest.

## How they judge WASTR

- **Evidence over claims:** validated outcomes, with source and sample size, not projections.
- **Scope of the grant:** only `lane: b2b` evidence is reported to Innovasjon Norge; `lane: b2c`
  data, revenue and consumer tests are never reported as a grant outcome.
- **Milestones:** what was promised, what was done, what changed and why.
- **Honesty about gaps:** what is unvalidated, what failed, what was decided against.
- **Team and runway** for investors; **public benefit** and method for grant bodies.

## Evidence they trust

Dated, sourced signals linked to issues; decisions with kill criteria; metrics with a frozen baseline;
third-party corroboration. Not: headline impact numbers that are internal projections.

## Typical objections

"Is this an outcome or an activity?" "How many sources?" "How does this differ from existing
solutions?" "What would make you stop?"

## Must not say or imply

Any figure presented as measured that is a projection (for example the empty-run or CO₂ headline
numbers); any outcome outside the funded scope; any stakeholder's confidential statements.

## Vocabulary

Milepæl, rapportering, resultatmål, tilskudd, validering. Say Innovasjon Norge in full.
