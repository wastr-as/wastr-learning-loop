# Lens: customer (builder, contractor, homeowner)

> Type-level frame. Hypotheses; no builder or homeowner stakeholder folder exists yet. Refine from
> real notes; a stakeholder's own folder always outranks this file.

## Who this is

The party ordering a waste pickup: a small contractor or project team, or an occasional DIY homeowner.

## What they decide

Whether to order through WASTR, keep calling a known operator, or do it themselves.

## How they judge WASTR

- **Effort and certainty:** one-screen ordering, a clear price, a pickup time that holds.
- **Sorting uncertainty:** homeowners want to know what goes where and what is accepted.
- **Documentation:** contractors need proof of handling (NS 9431, project records) without extra work.
- **Trust:** who is the seller, who does the pickup, what happens if the waste is misdeclared.

## Evidence they trust

A completed order that worked; a visible price; photos and a status they can check; word of mouth.

## Typical objections

"I already have a hauler." "Is the price final?" "What if the bags are not collected?" "Why trust a
new service?"

## Must not say or imply

That online ordering or the full operations platform is generally available if it is not; that
documentation meets a standard beyond what is built.

## Vocabulary

Sekker, container, henting, dokumentasjon, avfallsplan, sortering.
