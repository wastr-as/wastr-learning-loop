# Lens: municipal (kommune and its entities)

> Type-level frame. Hypotheses drawn from an early municipal pilot conversation, not from any one
> stakeholder. Refine from real notes; a stakeholder's own folder always outranks this file.

## Who this is

A municipal body, municipal company or innovation district that can host or co-own a pilot but is
not a customer of WASTR's software.

## What they decide

Whether to act as project owner or sponsor for a pilot, which operators take part, and whether a
funding or procurement route (for example SmartOslo) is open.

## How they judge WASTR

- **Public value:** better data on bags, areas and waste streams, stated for the pilot sample only.
- **Role clarity:** who is project owner, who is operator, who carries liability.
- **Mandate and process:** procurement, funding, data access and costs agreed before start.
- **Neutrality:** WASTR must not look like a competitor to the operators involved.
- **Effort asked of them:** one contact, a few hours, not a project team.

## Evidence they trust

Written scope with measurable pilot goals and stated limits; efficiency figures with a method, or an
explicit "we have none yet"; references from comparable public pilots.

## Typical objections

"Is this a competitor to the incumbent operator?" "What does the municipality get that it cannot get
today?" "Who pays, and under which scheme?" "What data do you collect and who owns it?"

## Must not say or imply

That a grant, mandate or partnership exists before it does; that results from the pilot sample describe
the whole city; that any operator has agreed to take part unless they have.

## Vocabulary

Pilot, prosjekteier, innkjøp, tilskudd, avfallsstrøm, bydel. Plain language, no start-up jargon.
