## Commercial and Data Boundaries

- Relevant, profitable third-party assignments with little additional administration may be attractive. This is conditional interest, not a buying commitment.
- Any price must cover execution costs, administration and deviation risk. A fee or commission requires concrete job-level contribution calculations; no acceptable percentage was supplied.
- The private acquisition-cost estimate concerns obtaining waste/moving assignments. It is not a measured company-wide customer acquisition cost, an available WASTR budget or an acceptable fee.
- For its existing subcontracting workflow, the operator first checks capacity, then shares only details needed for the specific assignment. It retains the customer relationship and customer invoicing; the subcontractor invoices the operator.
- Broad access to existing customers or historical order data is not offered. Openness to necessary job-level information sharing does not establish consent to secondary analytics, resale or model training.
- **Keep two commercial models separate:** subcontracting the operator's existing jobs versus supplying genuinely new WASTR-originated jobs. Pricing authority, contracting, invoicing, support, repeat customers and permitted data use for the second model remain unresolved.

## Interpretation for WASTR

1. **First hypothesis: incremental, well-prepared demand may be more valuable than replacement software.** The operator reports a demand constraint and functioning tools. Validate incremental contribution and acceptable handoff before building an integration or proposing a subscription.
2. **Trust and job economics are qualification gates.** A technically easy order transfer is not enough if the customer sees loss of commercial control or unclear liability.
3. **Intake quality is a plausible operational opportunity.** Photos and structured access details may reduce clarification work. This response does not quantify that work or demonstrate willingness to pay for reducing it.
4. **Reporting is not yet the leading commercial case for this account.** Available records are not the same as customer demand for a reporting product. Do not interpret the unanswered social-value question as either demand or rejection.

This account is directionally consistent with the digitised-operator hypothesis in [Hente positioning](../../hente-no/analysis/2026-10-21_positioning.md) and [pilot learnings](../../../pilot-learnings.md). The operators and evidence sources differ; this response alone does not validate the hypothesis, invalidate the wider manual-operations segment, or establish a product pivot.
