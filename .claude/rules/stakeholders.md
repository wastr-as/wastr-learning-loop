# Stakeholder and market files

- This repository is public. Everything committed here must be publishable.
- Quotes verbatim, in the original language; everything else labelled `inference:`.
- Unnamed stakeholders stay unnamed: roles, not names; no contact details or financial figures.
- Never create or commit files under `transcripts/`.
- File names follow `YYYY-MM-DD_<type>-<topic>.md`; front matter keys are in
  `docs/stakeholders/README.md`.
- A stakeholder's folder holds that stakeholder's facts only. Do not copy another stakeholder's
  information into it, and do not cite one stakeholder's notes in another's folder.
